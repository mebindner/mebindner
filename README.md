#### Sea lice in Norwegian salmon farming · [live app](https://lakselus.streamlit.app) · [code](https://github.com/mebindner/lakselus)

[![Time machine: adult female lice per fish at every Norwegian fish farm](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mebindner/lakselus/main/docs/social-preview.png)](https://lakselus.streamlit.app)

A weekly brief, a 3D time machine and a 1–4 week forecast for all 13 Norwegian production areas, built on open
BarentsWatch data. Gradient boosting cuts the forecast error by 8% one week ahead and 27% four weeks ahead
versus the naive forecast on held-out data from 2025 onwards (Diebold–Mariano tested). The app updates itself
every Wednesday via GitHub Actions.

*Python · pandas · scikit-learn · Streamlit · deck.gl*

#### Geospatial · maps from open data, made for fun · [code](https://github.com/mebindner/geospatial)

[![Sognefjord in 3D: sea lice per salmon farm, week 42 2026](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mebindner/geospatial/main/02-fjords-sea-lice/output/sognefjord_2026_final.png)](https://github.com/mebindner/geospatial/tree/main/02-fjords-sea-lice)

A series of maps built from open data, one project at a time. First up: the Sognefjord in 3D, where every
salmon farm is a bar sized and coloured by its weekly sea lice count. In week 42, all three farms above the
limit sat at the mouth of the fjord.

*R · rayshader · sf · terra*
